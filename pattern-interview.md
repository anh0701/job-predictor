# Some ## Patterns for interview

## Patrern 1: Giới thiệu bản thân

```sh
I am a [position] with [X years] of experience.
My main responsibilities include [responsibility].
Recently, I have been focusing on [skill].
Now, I am looking for an opportunity to [goal].
```

- example:

```sh
I am a Java developer with one year of experience.
My main responsibilities include developing backend applications and working with database.
Recently, I have been focusing on learning recruitment and talent acquisition.
Now, I am looking for an opportunity to transistion into IT Recruitment.
```

## Pattern 2: Tại sao muốn chuyển việc

```sh
I am looking for a new opportunity because [reason].

I would like to [goal].

I believe this position can help me achieve that.
```

- example:

```sh
I am looking for a new opportunity because I want to move into IT Recruitment.

I would like to combine my technical background with recruitment skills.

I believe this position can help me achieve that.
```

## Pattern 3: Điểm mạnh

```sh
One of my strengths is [strength].

For example, [example].

As a result, [result].
```

- example:

```sh
One of my strengths is communication.

For example, I often worked as a bridge between business analysts and developers.

As a result, project requirements were delivered more clearly.
```

## Pattern 4: Điểm yếu

```sh
One area I am working on is [weakness].

To improve this, I [action].

I have already seen progress.
```

- example:

```sh
One area I am working on is public speaking.

To improve this, I practice English speaking every day.

I have already seen progress in my confidence.
```

## Pattern 5: Vì sao công ty nên chọn bạn

```sh
I believe I can bring [value].

My background in [background] allows me to [benefit].

In addition, I am [quality].
```

- example:

```sh
I believe I can bring both technical understanding and recruitment potential.

My background in software development allows me to communicate effectively with engineers.

In addition, I am eager to learn and highly motivated.
```

## Pattern 6: Kinh nghiệm dự án

```sh
In this project, I was responsible for [task].

My role involved [activity].

The project helped me improve [skill].
```

- example:

```sh
In this project, I was responsible for backend development.

My role involved building APIs and working with databases.

The project helped me improve my problem-solving skills.
```

## Pattern 7: Mô tả công nghệ

```sh
I have experience with [technology].

I used it to [purpose].

It helped us [result].
```

- example:

```sh
I have experience with Spring Boot.

I used it to develop RESTful APIs.

It helped us build scalable backend services.
```

## Pattern 8: Trả lời khi không biết

```sh
I am not completely familiar with that topic.

However, based on my understanding, ...

I would be happy to learn more about it.
```

- example:

```sh
I am not completely familiar with that framework.

However, based on my understanding, it is used for building web applications.

I would be happy to learn more about it.
```

## Pattern 9: Đặt câu hỏi cho nhà tuyển dụng

```sh
Could you tell me more about ...

What does success look like in this role?

What are the biggest challenges for this position?
```

## Pattern 10: Kết thúc phỏng vấn

```sh
Thank you for your time.

I am very interested in this opportunity.

I look forward to hearing from you.
```
